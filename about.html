About Us

Maple's Dog Grooming started with a simple idea: every dog deserves to feel calm, comfortable, and cared for during grooming — not rushed through it. What began as grooming appointments for neighbors and friends has grown into a full-service studio built on the same philosophy: patient hands, low-stress handling, and genuine attention to each dog's individual needs.

Our Approach

We believe grooming should be a positive experience, not a stressful one. Every appointment is paced to the dog in the chair — whether that's a confident regular or a nervous first-timer — using gentle handling, frequent breaks, and plenty of reassurance along the way.
